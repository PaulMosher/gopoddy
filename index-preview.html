The problem isn't just the bag

We solved the wrong half.

The bag was never the solution — it was the workaround. A 2022 peer-reviewed study (Mai et al., Environmental Pollution) estimated 82 billion plastic dog waste bags consumed annually across North America. Bags are only the most visible part of the problem — dog waste also pollutes waterways, threatens public health, and represents over a billion dollars in renewable energy and fertilizer value sent straight to landfills.

The Poddy is the only product designed to address all three. It eliminates the bag, routes waste to the wastewater system the EPA recommends, and turns a daily nuisance into something better for your walk, your community, and the planet.

A note on the numbers: the bag estimate above comes from Mai et al., 2022 (Environmental Pollution), covering all of North America. Resource-value and adoption math throughout the site is built on the ~72 million suburban and urban dogs in the U.S. that represent the realistic addressable market for a collection system like the Poddy.

How it works

The lid is the scoop.

1Scoop

The scooper is integrated into the lid. Waste lifts cleanly — no hand contact, no thin bag between you and the problem.

2Seal

The lid screws closed and traps the air, keeping odor in. Carry it home clipped to your belt, leash, or bag.

3Empty

Tip the contents into the toilet — the EPA-recommended method. Rinse. Done. Ready for the next walk.

Field notes

Six years of use.
Still only one dog.

Teddy and I have carried a Poddy every day since 2020, through thirteen prototypes. That's the longest run of real use this product has — and it's still just one dog, one neighborhood. We're the sample size of one that got this far; the rest of the story needs other people, other dogs, other routines.

Version fourteen exists because of things we only found by using it — in rain, in snow, with gloves on, at 6am when neither of us felt like it.

— Paul, Mosher Group
