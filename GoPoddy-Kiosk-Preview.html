The problem

Bowl to bowl

It was never waste. It was nutrients taking a detour.

A daily readout
Stool is the clearest daily signal of how your dog is actually doing — it's why Purina publishes a fecal scoring chart. Good nutrition produces a clean lift. The Poddy makes checking it a habit instead of a chore.
Somewhere better to go
Flush it where your sewage system allows. Compost it where facilities exist. The lid is built to sit steady on a toilet rim — the disposal path was designed in, not bolted on.
People who already do this
Professional waste services handle this at scale every day. The Poddy fits alongside what they already do rather than competing with it.

Field notes

One Pup.
Two People.
Many Poddies.
Bags, Zero.

One of us has carried a Poddy every day since 2020, through thirteen prototypes. That's the longest run of real use this product has — and it's still only two people, one dog, two neighborhoods. They're both standing in front of you, so grade the sample accordingly.

What six years taught us
Version fourteen exists because of things we only found by using it — in rain, in snow, with gloves on, at 6am when we didn't feel like it.
What we're missing
Other people. Other dogs. Other diets, surfaces, climates and habits. No amount of our own use substitutes for that.
Which is where you come in
We brought a small sample. Some are going home with people willing to use one for 30–60 days and tell us the truth about it.
